Customisation & corporate branding

Files, binders and folders that carry your logo. Ideal for corporate gifting, client packs, school branding and event kits.

The process

From logo to delivered, in five steps.

1

Send your artwork

Share your logo as a vector file (AI, EPS, PDF) or a high-resolution PNG.

2

Choose product & finish

Pick the file, binder or folder, its colour, and a print method.

3

Approve the proof

We send a digital proof within one working day. Nothing prints until you approve.

4

We produce

Printing, foil or embossing in our production partners’ workshop. Typically 10 to 15 working days.

5

Delivered to you

Packed in cartons, labelled and delivered to your door.
